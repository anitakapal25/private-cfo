# Security and Personal-Data Incident Response

**Status:** Required operational workflow  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-30  
**Owner:** Incident commander designated by the security lead

1. Detect and open a timestamped incident record.
2. Classify severity, affected systems, data categories and users.
3. Contain access while preserving forensic evidence and chain of custody.
4. Rotate exposed credentials or keys and invalidate affected sessions.
5. Assess CERT-In, DPDP, contractual and sector-specific reporting duties using current official sources.
6. Notify decision owners and affected parties using approved templates and timelines.
7. Eradicate the cause, restore from verified artifacts and monitor recurrence.
8. Complete a blameless review with owned corrective actions and due dates.

Financial values, document contents, credentials and tokens must not be copied into
ordinary tickets or chat channels. Maintain tested 24-hour contact paths separately.
