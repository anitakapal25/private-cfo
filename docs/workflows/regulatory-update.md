# Regulatory and Public-Information Update Workflow

**Status:** Required operational workflow  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-30  
**Owner:** Compliance owner

1. Monitor official MeitY, CERT-In, RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA and Income Tax sources.
2. Record the publication, legal effective date and potentially affected features.
3. Obtain legal interpretation where product classification or user guidance may change.
4. Update the versioned rule catalogue and documentation.
5. Add or update calculation and guardrail tests.
6. Review affected historical outputs and user communications.
7. Approve, deploy and record the exact rule version.
8. Set the next mandatory review date.

Blogs and model memory may help discovery but are not authoritative sources.
